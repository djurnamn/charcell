as text

props

valuenumber
How far along, between min and max; the fill snaps down to whole cells. Left out, the bar is indeterminate.
minnumber= 0
The value at an empty track.
maxnumber= 100
The value at a full track.
labelstring
Names the bar, and leads the row in the mid gray.
showValueboolean
Prints the whole percent after the track.

it also takes any other attribute of its <div>.
